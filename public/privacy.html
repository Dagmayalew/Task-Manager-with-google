Privacy Policy for Chronotask

Effective Date: November 6, 2026 · Last Updated: November 6, 2026
Single Purpose Commitment: Chronotask is a personal productivity extension and task manager designed solely to help users create, organize, and synchronize their personal tasks with Google Tasks and block focus time on Google Calendar.

1. Overview and Core Principles

Chronotask ("the Extension", "we", "us", or "our") is designed with an offline-first architecture prioritizing user privacy. We do not sell your personal data, we do not show advertisements, and we do not track your internet browsing activities across websites.

2. Information Collected and Permissions Requested

To provide its core functionality, Chronotask utilizes the following permissions:

  • Alarms API (alarms): Used solely to schedule periodic background updates that refresh the extension icon badge count for tasks due today. It does not monitor or report your browsing behavior.
  • Storage API (storage): Used to cache your task lists, tasks, and calendar events locally on your device for offline productivity.
  • Identity API (identity): Used to facilitate secure client-side OAuth 2.0 authentication directly with Google Accounts when you choose to connect your Google Tasks and Google Calendar.
  • Side Panel API (sidePanel): Used to allow docking the task manager alongside web pages in Google Chrome for personal note-taking and task capture.
  • Host Permissions (https://tasks.googleapis.com/* & https://www.googleapis.com/*): Used solely to make authorized API requests to Google Tasks API and Google Calendar API to synchronize your tasks and schedule time-blocks when you sign in. No other external hosts are accessed.

3. How We Use and Store Data

  • Local Storage: All tasks, categories, and settings created in the application are stored locally on your device using browser storage.
  • Google Synchronization: When you connect your Google Account, your tasks and calendar events are transmitted directly between your browser and Google's official servers via encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • No Central Data Collection: We do not operate external third-party tracking servers that collect or aggregate your task content, names, or schedule data.

4. Compliance with Google API Services User Data Policy

Chronotask's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • We never use Google Workspace data (Google Tasks or Google Calendar) for serving advertisements.
  • We never sell or transfer Google user data to data brokers.
  • We never use your data for training machine learning or artificial intelligence models.

5. Your Rights and Data Deletion

You have complete control over your data:

  • You can delete individual tasks or entire lists at any time through the application interface.
  • You can sign out of your Google Account at any time, which immediately terminates and discards active access tokens.
  • Uninstalling the extension from Google Chrome immediately removes all locally cached data from your computer.

6. Developer Certification & Inquiries

We certify that our collection and use of user data strictly complies with the Chrome Web Store Developer Program Policies.
